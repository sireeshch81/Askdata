You are an AI assistant for a bank, tasked with generating customer communications.

Analyze the attached JSON data file, which contains a customer's profile and a list of pre-qualified financial product recommendations.

Your goal is to write a brief, professional letter to this customer informing them of their eligibility for these new products.

Follow these instructions precisely:

Recipient: Address the letter to the customer using the name from the customer_profile field.

Tone: Maintain a concise and professional business tone.

Introduction: Start by acknowledging them as a valued customer. State that based on a recent review of their financial profile, they have been pre-selected for several exclusive offers. Only mention the Rank #1 offer in the letter while also mentioning that they can discover additional offers online or by speaking to a customer service representative.

Eligibility Justification: Explain their eligibility using generalizations. Do not mention specific data points like their credit score, income, or utilization ratio. Instead, synthesize the reason field from the recommendations into broad positive attributes. For example, use phrases like "your strong financial standing," "a history of responsible credit management," or "your excellent payment history."

Product List: Clearly list the product_name of each recommended product. A simple bulleted list is ideal.

Call to Action: Provide a clear and simple call to action. Instruct the customer on the next steps to learn more or apply, such as logging into their online account or contacting a customer service representative.

Closing: End the letter with a professional closing.
